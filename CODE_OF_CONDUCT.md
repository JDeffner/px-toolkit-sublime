# Code of Conduct

This code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, wiki contributions, and other discussions hosted by this project. Contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully and discuss the work rather than attacking the person.
- Give useful context, accept corrections, and make room for newcomers.
- Respect the maintainer's time. Delayed replies do not justify repeated demands or harassment.
- Respect privacy and share only information you have permission to disclose.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a concern that can be described safely in public, ask the maintainer to review the relevant discussion. Do not post private details or repeat abusive material unnecessarily. For sensitive reports, or if the concern involves the maintainer, use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting route](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock a discussion, or restrict participation. Responses should consider the impact, severity, and repeated behavior. A person affected by a moderation decision may ask for an explanation or reconsideration without continuing the disputed conduct.

This is a small volunteer project. Moderation is handled as availability permits, without a guaranteed response deadline. These rules govern participation in project spaces; they do not change the software license.
